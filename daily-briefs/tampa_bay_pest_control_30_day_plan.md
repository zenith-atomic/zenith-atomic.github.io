# Tampa Bay Pest Control Revenue Plan (30 Days)

## Objective
Build a repeatable outbound + follow-up system that books pest control discovery calls and closes at least one paid setup or retainer.

## Primary Offer
A follow-up system for pest control companies that recovers missed calls, old quotes, and review requests.

## Weekly Goals

### Week 1, Foundation and list build
- Define the ICP and offer positioning.
- Build a lead list of Tampa Bay pest control companies.
- Filter for companies with no website and/or fewer than 50 Google reviews.
- Prepare email, call, and follow-up templates.
- Goal metrics:
  - 100 qualified leads
  - 30 contacts validated
  - 10 personalized first-touch drafts

### Week 2, Outreach launch
- Send first-wave outreach by email.
- Start call follow-up for non-responders.
- Track replies, objections, and interest.
- Goal metrics:
  - 50 outbound emails sent
  - 20 calls made
  - 5 replies
  - 2 booked calls

### Week 3, Iteration and proof
- Review reply patterns and refine messaging.
- Test 2-3 subject lines and 2 call openers.
- Build a simple proof snapshot or mini audit for interested leads.
- Goal metrics:
  - 75 more emails sent
  - 25 calls made
  - 8 replies total for the week
  - 3 additional booked calls

### Week 4, Close and stabilize
- Push active prospects to decision.
- Offer a fixed-scope starter package.
- Systematize handoff and delivery.
- Goal metrics:
  - 3-5 sales calls held
  - 1-2 closes
  - 1 repeatable fulfillment checklist

## 30-Day Daily Breakdown

### Days 1-3
- Finalize offer and niche.
- Define qualification rules.
- Set up lead sheet columns.
- Draft email and call scripts.

### Days 4-7
- Build first lead batch.
- Manually verify websites and review counts.
- Tag prospects by type and urgency.
- Prepare first outreach batch.

### Days 8-10
- Send first emails.
- Start first call follow-up.
- Log every response.

### Days 11-14
- Review results.
- Tighten copy.
- Add clearer pain-specific hooks.

### Days 15-17
- Expand lead list.
- Send second wave.
- Call previous non-responders.

### Days 18-21
- Create mini audit examples.
- Use objections to refine pitch.
- Book and run calls.

### Days 22-24
- Push active leads toward close.
- Present one clear starter package.
- Track close reasons and stalls.

### Days 25-27
- Re-engage warm leads.
- Test a second angle if needed.
- Follow up twice on every hot lead.

### Days 28-30
- Close remaining prospects.
- Review metrics.
- Document what worked.
- Lock the next 30-day plan.

## Metrics That Matter
- Qualified leads added
- First-touch response rate
- Reply-to-call-booked rate
- Calls completed
- Close rate
- Revenue collected

## Full Scope
- Lead sourcing
- Manual verification
- Outreach drafting
- Call scripts
- Follow-up system
- Objection handling
- Simple offer delivery
- Tracking and reporting

## Tools Needed
- Google Sheets for lead tracking
- Gmail for outreach
- Google Docs for scripts and notes
- Google Maps / Google Business Profiles for prospect discovery
- A phone line or call app
- Optional: lightweight CRM

## Notes
- Keep the offer narrow.
- Use real local proof and specifics.
- Avoid generic AI-sounding language.
- Call after email if there’s no reply.
