# Morning AI brief source checklist

1. Anthropic news/blog: https://www.anthropic.com/blog — official Anthropic newsroom for Claude and research updates.
2. OpenAI news/blog: https://openai.com/newsroom — official OpenAI newsroom for product, research, and safety updates.
3. Google AI / Gemini blog: https://blog.google/products/gemini/ — official Gemini news hub on Google Blog.
4. Reuters AI/tech (optional): https://www.reuters.com/world/us/technology/ — optional wire source for broader AI and tech coverage.

Note: the main brief job should fetch these URLs directly, and fall back to the official pages if live search is noisy.
