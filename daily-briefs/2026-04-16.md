- Anthropic: Anthropic expanded its partnership with Google and Broadcom for multiple gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity, expected online starting in 2027. Why it matters: compute supply is still a core moat in frontier AI, and this signals Anthropic is locking in long-run scale. Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute

- OpenAI: OpenAI said a third-party Axios library compromise touched its macOS app-signing workflow, but it found no evidence of user data access or product tampering and is rotating certificates. Why it matters: supply-chain and app-signing security are now part of the competitive trust surface for AI vendors, especially on desktop. Source: https://openai.com/index/axios-developer-tool-compromise/

- Google Gemini: Google says Gemini 3 is the latest major update for the Gemini app, with stronger reasoning and broader rollout across the Gemini app, Search, AI Studio, Vertex AI, and Google Antigravity. Why it matters: Google is pushing Gemini deeper into its product stack, which keeps distribution and workflow integration as its main advantage. Source: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3/

Why it matters: the signal today is concentrated in compute, security, and model/product rollout rather than a broader market-shifting event. Source: https://openai.com/newsroom
