Pre-market market-conditions brief for Nicolas
Date: 2026-04-16

1) Commodities
- Move: U.S. crude, gasoline, and distillate inventories fell in the latest EIA report, while oil stayed supported by Iran / Strait of Hormuz supply-risk headlines.
- Why it matters: Tight fuel balances keep energy inflation, transport costs, and volatility elevated, which can spill into consumers and margin-sensitive businesses.
- Source: EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report, https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/ ; Reuters on oil and Iran risk, https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-prices-fall-for-a-second-day-on-expectations-usiran-talks-may-resume-4614155

2) Tech
- Move: Nasdaq hit an intraday and closing record yesterday as investors rotated back into tech; Netflix reports Q1 results this afternoon.
- Why it matters: The tape is rewarding mega-cap duration again, but Netflix is a live read on consumer demand, pricing power, and ad-tier momentum before the open risk fully settles.
- Source: Reuters via Investing.com, https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nasdaq-sets-record-highs-as-investors-return-to-tech-stocks-4616614 ; Netflix IR, https://ir.netflix.net/investor-news-and-events/financial-releases/press-release-details/2026/Netflix-to-Announce-First-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx

3) Treasury policy / rates / yields
- Move: Official Treasury/Fed yield data were broadly steady into today, with the front end still elevated and the long end near the mid-4% area; the next FOMC is Apr. 28-29.
- Why it matters: Stable yields mean the market is still waiting for clearer policy direction, and valuation sensitivity stays high for long-duration assets.
- Source: U.S. Treasury daily yield curve, https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value=2026&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve ; Fed calendar, https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm

4) Geopolitics
- Move: Meaningful geopolitical driver remains U.S.-Iran negotiations, with Reuters reporting oil sold off on expectations talks may resume.
- Why it matters: Any deal or de-escalation could quickly unwind part of the energy risk premium, while failure keeps crude and hedging demand bid.
- Source: Reuters via Investing.com, https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-prices-fall-for-a-second-day-on-expectations-usiran-talks-may-resume-4614155

CEO takeaway
- Keep it defensive but not gloomy, respect energy spillover risk, and use tech strength selectively until rates and Netflix give a cleaner read on risk appetite.
