# Board Memo — AI Market Update

Date: 2026-04-04
Audience: CEO / board / investors

## 1. Executive Take
AI is moving from broad experimentation to production deployment. The important shift is structural, not cosmetic: frontier model capability is improving, capital is concentrating around a few dominant players, and enterprise distribution ecosystems are hardening. This increases pressure on generic AI product features and favors SaaS businesses that own a specific workflow with measurable ROI.

## 2. What Changed
- OpenAI continues pushing frontier capability and enterprise positioning with GPT-5 and GPT-5.4.
- AI fundraising remains historically large, increasing the likelihood of aggressive bundling and pricing competition.
- Anthropic is investing heavily in enterprise partnership/distribution infrastructure.
- The market narrative is shifting from novelty to monetization, compliance, and deployment.

## 3. Why It Matters To Us
- Generic AI features are becoming easier to copy.
- Product defensibility will increasingly come from workflow ownership, integrations, data advantage, and operational trust.
- Model vendors are becoming stronger platform companies, which raises both leverage opportunities and dependency risk.

## 4. Risks
- Feature commoditization
- Vendor dependency on a small number of model providers
- Margin compression if pricing follows the large labs downward
- Enterprise buyer expectations rising faster than product maturity

## 5. Opportunities
- Build narrow AI workflow products with clear business outcomes
- Package reliability, governance, and integration as a premium capability
- Use model improvements to reduce delivery cost and increase product quality without changing headcount at the same rate

## 6. Recommended Actions (30 Days)
1. Audit all AI features and classify them as commodity vs differentiating.
2. Choose one painful workflow to optimize deeply.
3. Add ROI instrumentation around time saved, conversion, retention, or cost reduction.
4. Reassess vendor concentration risk and fallback options.
5. Prepare a clearer customer-facing story around reliability, governance, and data handling.

## 7. Bottom Line
The market is rewarding focused execution, not generic AI wrappers. The best path is to embed AI deeply into a painful workflow and make the product more valuable than the underlying model.
