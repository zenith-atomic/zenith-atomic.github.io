# AI Daily Brief for Nicolas

Date: 2026-04-12

## 5-bullet CEO brief

- Anthropic is shifting the AI race from model demos to secured capacity. It announced a multi-gigawatt next-generation TPU deal with Google and Broadcom, with capacity starting in 2027, while saying run-rate revenue has surpassed $30B and enterprise customers spending $1M+ annually doubled from 500 to 1,000 in under two months. CEO read: the moat is now compute plus distribution, not just model quality. Action: revisit infrastructure exposure and partner concentration before suppliers tighten further. (Anthropic, Apr 6)

- Anthropic also raised the floor on premium model capability, not just the ceiling. Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 add 1M-token context windows, stronger agentic coding/computer-use performance, and keep prior pricing tiers. CEO read: more enterprise workflows that previously needed custom software can now be automated with cheaper, more reliable general models. Action: identify 2-3 internal analyst, operations, or spreadsheet-heavy workflows to re-cost against current frontier-model economics. (Anthropic product announcements)

- Google is pushing a serious open-model flank with Gemma 4. Google says the family is Apache-licensed, tuned for reasoning and agentic workflows, and efficient enough to run from Android devices to workstations; Android Developer Blog separately positions it as the base for next-gen on-device Gemini Nano 4. CEO read: local-first AI is becoming commercially viable for privacy-sensitive and cost-sensitive products. Action: pressure-test whether one roadmap item should move from API-dependent to hybrid local deployment. (Google Blog, Apr 2; Android Developers Blog)

- Meta is changing tactics and going more closed in pursuit of relevance. CNBC reports Meta launched proprietary model “Muse Spark,” the first major model from Meta Superintelligence Labs under Alexandr Wang, while guiding 2026 AI capex to roughly $115B-$135B. CEO read: even Meta appears willing to trade open-positioning for speed and product control, which is a signal that monetization pressure is overtaking open ecosystem optics. Action: assume platform behavior will get more vertically integrated and protect against dependency on any single lab’s access terms. (CNBC, Apr 8)

- OpenAI’s macOS signing issue is a reminder that AI risk now includes supply-chain trust, not just model behavior. Reuters and The Verge report OpenAI responded to a broader Axios package compromise that affected a GitHub Actions workflow used in macOS app signing, prompting certificate updates tied to ChatGPT Desktop, Codex, Codex-cli, and Atlas. CEO read: AI adoption risk increasingly sits in the tooling stack around models. Action: audit CI/CD, package provenance, and code-signing workflows anywhere AI tools are entering the developer environment. (Reuters, Apr 11; The Verge)
