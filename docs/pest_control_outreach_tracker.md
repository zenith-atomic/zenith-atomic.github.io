# Pest Control Outreach — Cadence Tracker & Log

## Cadence Per Lead

| Day | Action | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Day 1 | Send email (Template A, B, or C) | Personalize with review count and city |
| Day 2 | Call | Reference the email, don't assume they read it |
| Day 4 | Follow-up email | Short — just circling back |
| Day 7 | Call again | Different time of day than first call |
| Day 10 | Final email bump | Close the loop, leave the door open |

After Day 10 with no response → move to "Dormant" status. Re-engage in 30 days.

---

## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Not contacted | On the list, not reached yet |
| Email sent | First email sent, waiting |
| Called — VM | Left voicemail, no response |
| Called — reached | Spoke with someone |
| Replied | Got an email reply |
| Interested | Expressed interest, scheduling call |
| Call booked | Discovery call on calendar |
| Not interested | Declined clearly |
| Wrong person | Need to find decision maker |
| Dormant | No response after full cadence |
| Closed | Paid client |

## Reply Handling Quick Reference

| Reply Type | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Tell me more" | Book the 15-min call immediately. Send calendar link. |
| "Send me info" | Send the offer sheet PDF or one-pager. Follow up in 2 days. |
| "Not interested" | "Understood — can I ask why? Just want to improve." |
| "Maybe later" | "When's a better time?" Lock a date or a month. |
| "How much?" | "Depends on your setup — that's what the call is for. 15 mins?" |
| No reply | Move to next cadence step per schedule above. |
