# Pest Control ICP & Lead Qualification Rules

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Geography
- Tampa Bay area (Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Wesley Chapel, Lakeland, Sarasota corridor)
- Focus on suburbs and mid-market cities first — not downtown Tampa (lower owner-operated density)

### Business Profile
- Owner-operated or small multi-crew (1–25 employees)
- General pest control or residential + commercial mix
- Revenue model: service calls, recurring treatments, one-off jobs
- Not a national franchise chain (Terminix, Orkin, etc.) — skip these

### Pain Signals (what to look for in research)
- Fewer than 50 Google reviews → high priority
- No website or website is thin (no booking form, no clear call to action)
- Slow response time on calls (can test by calling during business hours)
- No review response pattern (owner doesn't reply to reviews)
- No obvious follow-up system visible (no chat widget, no autoresponder)

---

## Priority Tiers

### Tier A — Best fit (contact first)
All of:
- Under 50 Google reviews
- No website or website lacks booking/contact form
- Owner-operated (findable on Google Maps, reviews mention owner by name)
- In core Tampa Bay metro

### Tier B — Good fit
Any 2 of:
- 50–100 Google reviews (still room to grow)
- Website exists but has no online booking or form
- Business is 3+ years old (more stale leads to reactivate)

### Tier C — Possible fit (lower priority)
- 100–200 reviews (may already have systems)
- Franchise or heavily branded (harder sell)
- Outside core Tampa Bay geography

---

## Disqualify If
- National chain (Terminix, Orkin, Massey, Rentokil)
- No phone number findable (unreachable)
- Permanently closed or out of business
- Reviews are all negative and business looks like it's failing
- Already running obvious automation (chat widget, instant response, perfect review cadence)

---

## How to Research Each Lead

1. Search Google Maps: "pest control [city]" — work through the results
2. Open their Google Business Profile:
   - Record review count and star rating
   - Note if they respond to reviews
   - Note if they have a website linked
3. Open their website (if they have one):
   - Do they have a contact/booking form?
   - Does the site look maintained (updated content, photos, date)?
   - Is there a chat widget or obvious follow-up flow?
4. Call the number (optional but powerful):
   - Does anyone pick up?
   - Do they call back if you leave a voicemail?
   - How fast?
5. Score and assign tier (A/B/C)

---

## Lead Sheet Entry Protocol

When adding a lead to the tracker:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|--------------|
| Company Name | Exact Google Business name |
| Owner/Contact | Owner name if findable in reviews or website "About" |
| Phone | Primary phone from GBP |
| Email | From website contact page or Google listing |
| Website Y/N | Y if they have a real site, N if none or just a GBP page |
| Google Reviews | Count from GBP at time of research |
| Google Maps URL | Direct URL to their GBP listing |
| City | Their primary service city |
| Priority | A / B / C per tier rules above |
| Status | "Not contacted" when first added |

---

## Week 1 List-Build Target
- 100 qualified leads entered into the tracker
- At least 30 fully verified (phone, email, website, review count confirmed)
- At least 10 tagged as Tier A
- At least 10 personalized first-touch emails drafted and ready to send
