## Personalization Notes

- **Template A** → use for companies with weak web presence, no booking form, or that seem to rely purely on phone
- **Template B** → use for companies with decent traffic or ad spend but likely low follow-up discipline
- **Template C** → use when competitor review count gap is notable (>30 review delta)
- Always insert exact review count from Google Business Profile
- Reference their city specifically, not just "Tampa Bay"
- Keep sentences short — pest control owners are not sitting at a desk reading long emails
