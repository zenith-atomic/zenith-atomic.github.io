# Pest Control Research Test 1

## Targets
1. Cross Pest Control of Tampa
2. Turner Pest Control
3. Anti-Pesto Bug Killers

## Key Findings
### Cross Pest Control of Tampa
- Local pest-control brand with Tampa-specific positioning.
- Quick scan suggests room to improve conversion and lead follow-up.
- Best angle: missed-call recovery and turning site traffic into booked inspections.

### Turner Pest Control
- Established pest-control brand with a dedicated Tampa location page.
- FAQ-style content suggests a mature site, but speed-to-lead and follow-up are still likely revenue levers.
- Best angle: lead leakage reduction and response-time improvement.

### Anti-Pesto Bug Killers
- Strong local recognition and service/pricing information.
- More optimized than a small operator, so the outreach angle should be operational ROI, not basic website critique.
- Best angle: quote response, reactivation, and follow-up conversion.

## What the research agent should do next time
- identify the revenue lever first
- avoid generic website critique when the brand is already established
- surface an ROI hook in one line
- keep outputs short and actionable

## Next Step
Use these findings to draft a short outreach angle for each target and log them in the dashboard.
