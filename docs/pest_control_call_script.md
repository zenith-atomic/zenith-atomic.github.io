# Pest Control Cold Call Script

## Goal
Get 15-minute discovery call booked. Not to sell the full system on the phone — just to open the door.

---

## Before Calling
- Know the company name
- Know their Google review count
- Know whether they have a functioning website
- Know which city they serve
- Have their email ready in case they ask you to send something

---

## Opener (First 10 seconds)

"Hi, is this [Owner First Name]?"

*[Yes]*

"Hey [Name], my name is Nicolas. I'm not trying to sell you anything right now — I just have a quick question. Do you have 30 seconds?"

*[Yes or they don't hang up]*

"When someone calls your business and nobody picks up — what happens to that lead?"

---

## Pain Development (Let Them Talk)

Listen for:
- "We try to call back when we can"
- "We miss some yeah"
- "My office handles it"
- "Honestly some fall through the cracks"

If they say everything is fine: "Good — most guys I talk to in pest control say the same thing until we look at the actual numbers. That's usually where the gap shows up."

---

## The Hook (30 seconds)

"The reason I'm calling is I work with pest control companies in Tampa Bay specifically on one thing — recovering revenue from leads they already paid for. Missed calls, quotes that go cold, customers who need a reminder. It all runs automatically so your team doesn't have to chase anything."

"I'm not a marketing agency. I don't run ads. I just focus on what's already coming in and make sure it converts."

---

## The Ask

"I know this is out of the blue. I'm not asking you to commit to anything — I just want to show you what this looks like in 15 minutes on a call. If it doesn't fit, no problem."

"Do you have 15 minutes this week or next week I could grab?"

---

## Common Objections

**"I'm not interested."**
"Totally fine. Quick question before I let you go — is it that the timing's off, or you already have something handling follow-up?"
→ If timing: "Can I reach back out in [X weeks]?"
→ If handled: "What are you using? Just curious."

**"Send me an email."**
"Happy to. What's the best email?" [Get it, confirm it]
"I'll send something short — and I'll follow up in a couple days to see if it's worth a call."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"Depends on what you need — typically a setup fee around $1,500 and a small monthly. But that's exactly what the call is for — I don't quote anything until I know what you're working with."

**"We're busy right now."**
"Completely understand — pest season, right? When does it slow down a bit for you? I can reach out then."

**"Who are you with?"**
"I run a small operation that works specifically with home service companies in Tampa. Not a big agency — just focused on one problem."

---

## Close

After booking:
"Perfect. I'll send a calendar invite to [email]. If something comes up just shoot me a text — [number]. Looking forward to it."

---

## After the Call — Log Immediately
- Date and time of call
- Who you reached (owner, office, voicemail)
- Response: interested / not now / wrong person / voicemail
- Next follow-up action and date
- Any details they mentioned (pain points, current setup, competitor mentions)
