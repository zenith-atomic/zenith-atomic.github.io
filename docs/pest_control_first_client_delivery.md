# Pest Control First Client Delivery Workflow

## Objective
Get the first client live fast, prove revenue lift, and convert the pilot into a paid ongoing account.

## Step 1, Qualify
- local pest control company
- low review count or weak profile
- slow / thin website
- no clear booking flow
- owner cares about more booked jobs

## Step 2, Sell the Promise
- recover missed calls
- follow up on quotes
- reactivate old leads
- request more reviews
- make the owner see lost revenue

## Step 3, Install
- collect business data
- load templates
- turn on sequences
- verify notifications
- test logging

## Step 4, Measure
Track:
- missed calls recovered
- leads replied to
- quotes followed up
- bookings created
- reviews requested
- reviews received

## Step 5, Weekly Reporting
Send:
- what went live
- what responded
- what booked
- what needs tuning
- next actions

## Step 6, Convert
Offer:
- setup fee
- monthly management
- optional add-ons for extra locations, extra numbers, or more sequences

## Step 7, Reuse
Turn every install into:
- a template
- a checklist
- a reporting format
- a reusable offer
