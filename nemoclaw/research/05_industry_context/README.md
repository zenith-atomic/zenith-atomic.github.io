# Industry Context — Home Services

## Mission
Build background context on the home services industry to ground the product in real market dynamics.

## What to capture
- Market size (US home services industry ~$500B+)
- Number of SMBs in pest control, HVAC, plumbing
- Average lead response time expectations
- Industry-specific pain points
- Regulatory notes (licensing, insurance requirements by state)

## Sources
- IBISWorld (home services market reports)
- Statista
- Trade association data (NPMA, ACCA)
- BBB data
