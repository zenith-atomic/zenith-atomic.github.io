### Test Plan
- **Hypothesis 1, stat lead and contrarian take hooks outperform other styles on Twitter**  
  - **What we're testing:** Whether the analytics recommendation to lead with stat leads and contrarian takes actually moves engagement.  
  - **How we'll measure it:** Compare ER on stat lead posts vs non-stat-lead posts this week. Track quote-tweet count on contrarian take posts.  
  - **Success looks like:** Both stat lead posts finish above the 4.39% baseline. Contrarian take earns more than 2x the replies of neutral posts.

- **Hypothesis 2, explicit friction-and-wins framing drives higher trust signals than polished results-only posts**  
  - **What we're testing:** Whether naming the 429/503 errors and showing the workaround earns more "real" and "honest" comments than posts that only show the output.  
  - **How we'll measure it:** Comment sentiment analysis — specifically looking for comments using "real," "honest," "useful," or "this is how."  
  - **Success looks like:** At least 40% of comments on friction posts contain a trust signal word. No comments asking why the list is so small.

- **Hypothesis 3, LinkedIn carousel docs with a reframing argument outperform single-image posts on clicks and saves**  
  - **What we're testing:** Per the analytics recommendation to run a LinkedIn carousel as an experiment, test whether a carousel doc (Thursday) or a post (Monday) wins on clicks and saves.  
  - **How we'll measure it:** LinkedIn click count and save count on Thursday carousel doc vs Monday post.  
  - **Success looks like:** Thursday carousel doc generates at least 1.5x the clicks of Monday post. Saves are proportionally higher.
