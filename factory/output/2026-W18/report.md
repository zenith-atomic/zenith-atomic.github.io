### What Worked
1. **High-intent content.** Post 2026-W13-001 drove 23 clicks. Click content is rare in top-of-funnel campaigns. This post likely had a tutorial, tool reference, or a "here's how" angle — the kind that makes people bookmark instead of scroll.
2. **Engagement rate held above 4% on both posts.** For a new or low-follower account, this signals the algorithm is surfacing the content to the right audience. Not a fluke.
3. **Volume-to-impression ratio is healthy.** 1,500 impressions from a post with likely lower follower count suggests shareability and topic resonance, not just follower distribution.

### What Didn't Work
1. **No format tracking.** Cannot determine whether carousel, text, video, or image is driving results. This is the single biggest gap in the data.
2. **No pillar tagging.** Cannot attribute performance to Build in Public, Tool Reviews, Takes, or Behind the Scenes. Strategy cannot be optimized blind.
3. **Post 2026-W13-002 underperformed on both reach and engagement rate.** The LinkedIn post trailed the other post by 700 impressions and 0.22 percentage points of ER. Only one data point — monitor for pattern.

### Persona Evolution Recommendations
- **Voice:** Keep it direct and punchy. The 4.39% average ER suggests the tone is landing. No change.
- **Topic adjustments:** Add explicit tooling/build signals to every post. The click data (23 on post 001) shows utility-driven content performs. Lean into "here's what I built" and "here's the workflow" angles.
- **Format adjustments:** Start tracking format on every post immediately. Until then, no format changes can be justified.
- **Posting time:** Both posts logged at ~21:23–21:37 UTC. Insufficient to establish a pattern. Keep logging for 4+ weeks.
- **Hook style updates:** Move stat lead to `styles_that_work` once one post with a stat lead shows above-average ER. Currently no confirmed winners — build the evidence base first.
- **Audience targeting:** Content that reduces "shipping paralysis" is outperforming pure commentary. Double down on actionable, build-oriented posts.
