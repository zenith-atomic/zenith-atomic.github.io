### Test Plan
- **Hypothesis 1, continuity content (week 2 of a research thread) outperforms standalone research posts**  
  - **What we're testing:** Whether referencing last week's output and showing progression earns stronger engagement than fresh-start posts.  
  - **How we'll measure it:** Reply depth, quote tweets referencing week 1, follower-to-impression ratio.  
  - **Success looks like:** Higher thread completion rate and more inbound requests for the spreadsheet template.

- **Hypothesis 2, explicit limitation statements increase trust without reducing engagement**  
  - **What we're testing:** Whether naming the constraint (small list by design, Google blocks, low confidence on unverified) hurts or helps credibility.  
  - **How we'll measure it:** Comment sentiment, saves, and DM tone.  
  - **Success looks like:** Comments say "honest" or "real" rather than "why is the list so small." Saves outpace likes.

- **Hypothesis 3, artifact-driven CTAs (spreadsheet template) drive more qualified followers than soft asks**  
  - **What we're testing:** Whether offering a specific, usable output converts better than generic "follow for more."  
  - **How we'll measure it:** New follower count after the offer tweet, DM inbound with the word "template" or "spreadsheet," profile click rate.  
  - **Success looks like:** Measurable spike in follows and at least 2-3 DM requests for the artifact within 48 hours.
