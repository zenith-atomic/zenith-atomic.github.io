### Test Plan
- **Hypothesis 1, verification-first content outperforms generic lead-gen content**  
  - **What we’re testing:** Whether posts centered on “verified no-website business” outperform broad AI lead-gen advice.  
  - **How we’ll measure it:** Twitter engagement rate, replies, saves, profile clicks, LinkedIn dwell/comments.  
  - **Success looks like:** Higher-than-normal saves and replies on the verification posts, plus at least one inbound DM or comment asking for the workflow.

- **Hypothesis 2, contrarian lines increase reach without hurting trust**  
  - **What we’re testing:** Whether sharp takes like “one verified lead beats 50 sloppy ones” pull stronger engagement than neutral educational posts.  
  - **How we’ll measure it:** Impressions-to-engagement ratio, reply quality, and negative sentiment check.  
  - **Success looks like:** More replies and shares than baseline, with mostly constructive responses.

- **Hypothesis 3, process content builds authority faster than polished outcome content**  
  - **What we’re testing:** Whether behind-the-scenes posts about flaky extraction, filtering, and verification create more trust than clean summaries alone.  
  - **How we’ll measure it:** Comments mentioning credibility, watch time on video, and follow-through on process posts.  
  - **Success looks like:** Noticeable lift in comments that reference “useful,” “real,” or “actually helpful,” plus stronger retention on short-form video.
