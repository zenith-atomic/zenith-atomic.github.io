### Weekly Theme
**Mastering AI-Powered Dev Workflows: From Prompt Engineering to Real Metrics**  
This theme was chosen to address the biggest gaps and trends identified: demonstrating real-world speed gains with AI coding assistants, sharing deep prompt engineering tactics for code generation, and transparently showing failures and successes in AI-driven dev pipelines. It aligns with Zenith’s audience pain points of tool fatigue and shipping paralysis by offering concrete, actionable insights to build leverage faster.

### Notes for Writer

- Maintain Zenith’s direct, confident, slightly irreverent tone. Keep sentences short and punchy.  
- Prioritize hooks that provoke curiosity and position Zenith as a no-BS expert (bold claims, contrarian takes, stat leads).  
- Avoid corporate jargon, filler, or vague hype. Be brutally honest especially in behind-the-scenes posts.  
- Use minimal emojis, bold key points for emphasis, and keep formatting clean.  
- Focus on actionable insights with measurable outcomes—readers should walk away with clear next steps or data.  
- Avoid politics, rage bait, and engagement farming tactics. Keep authenticity front and center.
